BARRIER

How to Play

Stay on the grid

Both riders move continuously. Turn to cut off your opponent before a wall, a trail or the edge cuts you off. A head-on crash is a draw. Each round win earns one point.

Steering

Use the arrow keys, WASD, the direction pad, or swipe on the arena. You can turn at right angles. A U-turn is blocked.

Online duel

Create a room and send its link or code to a friend. The host starts each round when both players have joined. Amber is Player 1 and blue is Player 2.
